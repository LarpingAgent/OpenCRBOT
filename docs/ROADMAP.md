# OpenCRBOT roadmap

## Milestone 0 — ADB foundation (implemented; emulator validation pending)

- Discover compatible ADB, list/connect devices, capture a PNG.
- Target GPGPC Developer Emulator at `localhost:6520`.
- No root or patcher dependency.
- `opencrbot record` writes a bounded screenshot session with dimensions, timestamps and SHA-256 manifest.
- `contracts.py` defines evidence provenance and fail-closed proposal validation; it does not dispatch actions.

**Exit condition:** Developer Emulator is listed and repeatable screenshots can be captured with serial/time/hash metadata.

## Milestone 1 — Reproducible capture/replay (Windows Training Camp verified)

- Record bounded screenshot sessions and JSONL manifests.
- Track frame sequence, dimensions, monotonic timestamps, hashes and capture source.
- Replay verified Windows or ADB sessions with `replay` and analyze them offline with `analyze --session`.

**Exit condition:** a saved session can be replayed in recorded order and inspected without a running emulator. The Windows Training Camp session recorded on 20 November 2026 contains 600 frames from before the match through the result, and passed manifest integrity checks.

## Milestone 3 — ClashAI policy and offline evaluation

- Initial CP3 capture comparison without `my_next`: 176 frames comparable, 0 ClashAI plays, 35 baseline suggestions. Approximate history from YOLO track births did not change those decisions. A later same-match pilot with the optional Next reader is summarized below; keep inference offline until perception and policy behavior pass independent held-out captures.
- Implemented a pure, limited defensive-hint policy. It never accesses ADB and recommendations are not executable.
- Use `vegetableleaf/ClashAI` as the primary decision-maker and keep the defensive hints as the comparison baseline.
- Map calibrated GPGPC observations to ClashAI `BoardState`, including board orientation, deck, card vocabulary, and the fields required by the checkpoint.
- Use ClashAI's normalized x-right/y-down board frame. Map screen positions with a calibrated board transform and ground-contact points before comparing against simulator coordinates. The current arena-relative YOLO centers are visualization estimates.
- Supply elapsed match time to `BoardState.t_sec` and keep unknown hand slots at `-1`. Collect tower status and HP evidence separately. The upstream model expects six tower entries and the matching eight-card deck vocabulary.
- Added ClashAI-owned inference adapters for S1 and plain GenModel FV3, plus an offline session comparison command. The Colab generalist covers all eight recorded GPGPC deck identities and loads through the adapter. The initial 13-frame CP3 comparison skipped five frames and chose `wait` on all eight comparable frames; reconstructed replay-data metrics were `cell_tile_top1=0.0956` and card top-1 `0.3306`.
- Added an optional Next-card icon template reader. With the original CP3 hand geometry and tracked analysis, it recognized `my_next` in 164/176 policy-comparable frames. FV3 then recommended 94 plays and 82 waits; the defensive baseline produced 35 suggestions and 141 waits, identical to its previous 600-frame decisions. Agreement was 117/176. Separate manual holdouts now cover 16 frames from two matches, with 64/64 hand and 16/16 Next readings correct. No one has labeled action quality, and replay-reconstruction metrics remain low; keep live hints closed until a larger independent holdout and policy-quality evaluation pass.
- The defensive baseline can complete local RoyaleGym matches using `tools/evaluate_royalegym.py`. The runner records the engine's acceptance or rejection of each proposed play. This measures simulator behavior with ground-truth public observations.
- Added Gen FV3 support and same-seed comparison against the defensive baseline. The first report incorrectly supplied `my_next=-1`; after passing RoyaleGym's exact own next-card identity, Gen FV3 accepted 24/51/28 plays and won seeds 0–2, while the baseline lost all three. The corrected report repeated byte-identically (SHA-256 `fce331468fe0454a08bdd3494bbf3749d158fbd6706fa126c527e01194976afd`). This closes the reproducible simulator-evaluation gate against a weak seeded-random opponent, not live GPGPC readiness: the optional screen reader is still unvalidated on an independent match, and FirstLight has not been paired. See [checkpoint status](CHECKPOINTS.md).
- The downloaded FirstLight IL_Replay sample currently has play/ability events but no frame/state snapshots. Its hf_to_crawl.py CSV export is not directly compatible with pipeline.dataset_gen, which requires driven replay JSON containing frames and play_frames. Build/replay states through a compatible simulator collector before using these events for supervised training; see [checkpoint status](CHECKPOINTS.md).
- FirstLight's own full-match evaluator is available, but this machine has no configured FirstLight offline runtime/ADB device. Its five shipped V4 checkpoints fit that source; RoyaleFish `.pt` snapshots have mismatched strict contracts, and neither group is a ClashAI FV3 warm start. The Colab FV3 checkpoint has no optimizer state and the general trainer needs explicit warm-start support before continuing from it. Details and hashes are in [checkpoint status](CHECKPOINTS.md).

**Exit condition:** paired evaluation report, model/data manifest and repeatable results.

## Milestone 4 — Supervised action transport

- Implemented a Windows-only, opt-in Training Camp path: show preview; require the exact `PLAY TRAINING CAMP` phrase per suggested action; refresh the frame and recommendation; require fresh regulation time and a confidently read, unchanged Next card; validate battle/hand/elixir state, board/hand geometry and window bounds; send one card/placement click pair; then verify hand-slot replacement and elixir drop. After three confirmed inactive frames, save the result image and request a manual WIN/LOSS/DRAW/UNKNOWN label.
- Added `tools/run_cp7_training_camp.ps1` to wire the existing Colab checkpoint, deck, YOLO/card assets, clock/Next templates and GPGPC calibration into the supervised runner. It still requires an explicitly selected window and per-action confirmation.
- Each proposal writes a `confirmation_requested` event before waiting, then appends its confirmation/dispatch/verification result to `actions-*.jsonl`; the live observation and recommendation remain in the paired `live-*.jsonl`. Confirmed-action mode fails fast unless stdin is an interactive TTY. Unconfirmed proposals are skipped. Errors or inconclusive outcomes stop the runner.
- Added a 15-second per-action confirmation timeout. On timeout, record `confirmation_timeout` and stop the runner without clicking, so delayed terminal input cannot confirm a later proposal. Separate terminal TTY smoke checks confirmed line input and timeout; the game preview/action path remains unverified.
- After a transition verifies, record the exact accepted own card/tick/placement and feed it into the next S1/FV3 decision. The history encoder was smoke-checked against a saved observation; opponent-play history from live screenshots remains unavailable.
- Fixed Win32 input coordinates for GPGPC's 125% DPI scaling by switching the input thread to Per-Monitor DPI Aware V2 while dispatching physical window coordinates. Menu navigation and entry into Training Camp now work; card click and post-click verification are still unproven.
- Improved the hand reader with conservative ORB template matching and passed the known tower-rubble template through the CP7 wrapper. A live diagnostic frame recognized all four visible hand cards; this is a same-layout observation, not an independent-match accuracy result.
- Three live Training Camp attempts were captured on 21 November. Their result images show losses to Trainer Jonas; each action log is empty. The first preceded the DPI/input correction. The second stopped at the per-action prompt without sending its previewed Archers play. The third recorded 360 frames, passed `verify-session`, proposed Arrows on the opponent's left Princess Tower with no visible troop cluster, and reached a 3–0 result before any per-action confirmation arrived. These losses measure capture-to-result only, not policy play or policy quality. See `runs/cp7-training-camp-20261121-061358-346/run-summary.json`, `runs/cp7-training-camp-20261121-063427-515/run-summary.json` and [checkpoint status](CHECKPOINTS.md).
- Next: start a fresh Training Camp match, review each current preview, confirm only a suitable fresh proposal, and verify its hand-slot and elixir transition before continuing. Complete a match with verified action events and a visible result screen; stop on stale frames, ambiguous state, disconnect or unsupported layout.

**Exit condition:** every action in one full Training Camp match has a verified result event and rejected actions leave policy state unchanged.

## Milestone 5 — Learning and optional providers

- Add strategy tuning or compatible model serving using captured and offline data.
- Require held-out evaluation and rollbackable model manifests.
- Consider additional source providers only when their licenses, version fingerprints and capabilities are clear.

## Deliberately excluded from the baseline

- Copying unlicensed code or opaque checkpoints.
- Blind reuse of game-memory offsets across emulator/game versions.
- Treating opponent hidden hand/queue as visible state.
- Running GPGPCPatcher automatically from OpenCRBOT.
